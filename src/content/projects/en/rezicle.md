## The idea

**Rezicle** is a French non-profit specialised in acoustic recycling: dismantling, transporting and reassembling soundproof booths, acoustic treatment of spaces and manufacturing eco-friendly panels. The site presents all of their services, from technical work to artistic projects (music teaching, recording, mixing).

## Features

- **Services overview** — booth recycling, acoustic treatment, on-site measurements
- **Product catalogue** — the REZNO range (absorbing panels, REZTRAP, REZBASTRAP), panels salvaged from job sites
- **Rentals** — ReZiContainer Studio, live concert equipment
- **Photo galleries** — completed projects and job sites
- **Animations** — smooth page transitions, scroll reveals
- **Custom typography** — optimised loading via Nuxt Fonts
